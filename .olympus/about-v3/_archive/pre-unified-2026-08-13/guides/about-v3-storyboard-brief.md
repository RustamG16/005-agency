# About V3 visual storyboard — discussion set

Status: concept visualization only; not production media and not implementation approval.

## Shared visual language

- Convenium warm ivory, near-black, graphite and controlled deep cherry.
- Editorial European composition, disciplined grid, restrained serif/sans pairing.
- Full-width desktop section mockups at 16:9.
- High media presence without generic agency layouts.
- Every section has a distinct communication purpose and distinct motion idea.
- No invented Marija likeness. Marija-dependent areas use a deliberate faceless silhouette or empty reserved frame.
- No fabricated client metrics, awards, testimonials or finished proof.

## Storyboard sequence

1. **Faceless hero** — existing layered acrylic composition; Rustam left, Marija right; no face reveal.
2. **Founder reveal** — independent black/red stage; two silhouettes approach and separate into profile cards.
3. **A little like therapy** — Marija-led consultation; listening and clarification before design begins.
4. **We read between the lines** — evidence, language and ambiguity become a structured interpretation map.
5. **One accountable direction** — the two perspectives converge at one shared working surface.
6. **Three ways forward** — three genuinely different directions derived from one brief, followed by a selected state.
7. **Apollo build system** — public-safe view of a rigorous system without exposing internal logic.
8. **Coming next** — calm forward-looking capability horizon, not a services repetition.
9. **Proof** — Sonnwerk and Meridian only; provenance-aware before/proposed presentation.
10. **Inquiry** — concise founder-level project invitation and form.

## Approval rule

Discuss and revise these section mockups first. Lock the full sequence, media function and layout before opening a clean Google Flow production project or editing `/about-v3`.
