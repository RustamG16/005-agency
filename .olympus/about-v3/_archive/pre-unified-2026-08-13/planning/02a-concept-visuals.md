# About V3 concept visual studies

These three raster moodframes were generated with the built-in image-generation tool as low-cost Gate B decision aids. They are not production assets, exact layouts, factual founder portraits or implementation specifications. Generated people are anonymous placeholders and must be replaced by real founder media after concept selection.

## Concept 1 — The Private Briefing

![The Private Briefing](./concept-visuals/concept-1-private-briefing.png)

**Prompt summary:** Create a 16:10 premium editorial website moodframe using Convenium's cotton, noir and restrained cherry identity. Show an oversized boutique-studio thesis, factual evidence rail, two-founder editorial photograph, early dark showreel theatre, “what we listen for” section, asymmetric founder spread and compact closing invitation. Use a heavy grotesk, literary serif and neutral metadata sans. Avoid Symbol Studio's distinctive expression, previous About concepts, abstract 3D, fabricated proof and empty pinned space.

**What the frame tests:** Whether a discreet consultation metaphor can feel sufficiently dense, human and premium without a large client archive.

## Concept 2 — Two Lenses, One Direction

![Two Lenses, One Direction](./concept-visuals/concept-2-two-lenses.png)

**Prompt summary:** Create a 16:10 editorial website moodframe built around two complementary founder perspectives. Use a central agreement seam, paired founder statements, equal portrait weight, diptych showreel, brief/interpretation/shared-decision sequence, language-continuity map and a final merged inquiry plane. Preserve Convenium's palette and typography roles. Avoid sterile symmetry, flag-wall decoration, fake evidence, WebGL and previous About structures.

**What the frame tests:** Whether the two-founder relationship can become a coherent visual and information system rather than a conventional team section.

**Important limitation:** The generated frame assigns the two opening statements to anonymous placeholder people; it does not define the final Rustam/Marija side or responsibility wording.

## Concept 3 — The Studio Journal

![The Studio Journal](./concept-visuals/concept-3-studio-journal.png)

**Prompt summary:** Create a 16:10 art-book/editorial-annual website moodframe with a dark showreel cover, issue index, multi-column origin essay, portrait/document contact sheet, labelled evidence cabinet, explicitly fictional brief demonstration, typographic studio standards and compact invitation. Use disciplined provenance labels, cotton/noir sheets and cherry editor marks. Avoid chaotic collage, fake clients, unlabelled fictional proof, decorative 3D and Symbol Studio's identity.

**What the frame tests:** Whether dense, labelled founder and independent-work evidence can establish authority without relying on agency-client claims.

## Reading the studies

- Judge the experience model, density, hierarchy and media posture—not the generated copy, faces or individual interface details.
- The selected direction will be translated into an original responsive specification after Gate B.
- Production-media prompts, exact shot lists, showreel script and export requirements remain deferred until one concept is approved.
