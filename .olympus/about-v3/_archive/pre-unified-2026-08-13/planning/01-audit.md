# About V3 evidence audit

## Scope and evidence

- Routes/states inspected: Convenium Homepage, Services, Work and Contact; Rustam portfolio and CV; Symbol Studio live About reference and scraped audit. The existing `/about` and `/about-v2` pages were intentionally excluded.
- Breakpoints: adjacent Convenium pages sampled at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844; Symbol audit covers 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844.
- Evidence locations: `.olympus/about-v3/evidence/`, `symbol-studio-design-audit.md`, `audit-notes.json`, four supplied Symbol screenshots, `https://russolutions.netlify.app/` and `/cv`.
- Analytics available: none.
- Limits: Marija biography and media are not verified; the two additional screen recordings were described but not attached/located; portfolio/CV claims are self-published unless supported by the linked documents; no client research or conversion baseline exists.

## Current experience in one sentence

Convenium already explains what it offers, shows work and handles inquiries elsewhere, but it lacks a dedicated founder-and-relationship story that converts a newly launched studio’s real career evidence, multilingual discovery advantage and boutique working model into trust.

## What already works

- Convenium has a coherent global identity: fixed segmented header, responsive mobile menu, noir/cotton/cherry palette, Archivo Black/Newsreader/Inter typography and an oversized typographic footer.
- Existing Homepage, Services and Work routes already demonstrate breadth and contain usable project cover/loop media.
- Rustam’s portfolio supplies a strong portrait, a clear whole-pipeline narrative, four independent/real project links and career-document proof.
- The Contact page already defines a useful qualification model and honest response/fit language; its structure can be shared rather than recreated.
- The multilingual founder combination and psychology-informed discovery model are genuinely differentiating, provided the language and qualification claims are verified.

## Reference deconstruction

### Symbol Studio visual thesis

An information-rich editorial studio profile alternates generous light space, dense proof modules, media and dark conversion surfaces so every major scroll answers a new buyer question.

| Principle | Evidence | Why it works | How to transform it for Convenium | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Every section answers a new question | Symbol moves from thesis to why-us, team, method, client fit, proof and FAQ across an 18,344px About page. | Density feels useful rather than decorative because information changes with each chapter. | Give `/about-v3` unique chapters: origin, founder perspectives, multilingual interpretation, discovery conversation, collaboration behavior and founder evidence. Remove topics already owned by other routes. | Exact section order, headings, numbered cards and long agency-process copy. |
| Showreel as early proof | Dedicated labelled showreel plane with duration/play affordance appears as a major editorial stage. | It converts capability claims into immediate sensory evidence. | Place a 45–60s Convenium showreel after the boutique thesis/founder setup, using real project recordings and founder/process footage. | Symbol’s poster composition, time, media or layout proportions. |
| Alternating density and surface | Large whitespace precedes structured modules; later dark cross-links/footer compress the page. | Creates pacing, reset and anticipation without relying on empty pinned screens. | Use cotton editorial fields for reading, noir for showreel/proof/cross-links/form, and cherry only for action/state. | Symbol’s light-gray/orange identity and Framer transitions. |
| Process is explained through operational detail | Large split process modules pair a visual plane with substantial explanatory copy and expandable questions. | Buyers understand how collaboration will feel, not only what deliverables exist. | Explain the psychology-informed discovery session, language matching, decision ownership and review rhythm; keep service deliverables on Services. | Their three-pillar branding method, numerals and accordion styling. |
| Cross-links and footer complete the decision | Services/Projects split panel and oversized contact footer establish next paths. | Prevents a dead end and preserves route responsibilities. | Use one asymmetric Services/Work bridge plus a compact project-fit entry that shares the Contact system; retain Convenium’s own oversized footer. | Exact 50/50 black panels, arrow controls, orange accents and footer wording. |

## Keep / transform / avoid

- **Keep:** Convenium chrome, palette, type families, portfolio loops, verified founder credentials, portfolio/CV link, multilingual access and direct-founder model.
- **Transform:** Symbol’s information density into founder-specific usefulness; showreel into real capability proof; process modules into discovery/collaboration chapters; cross-links into a concise route bridge.
- **Avoid:** previous About concepts, abstract WebGL without meaning, fake client proof, agency-scale language, AI-first messaging, service/work duplication, Symbol’s exact visual expression and a second disconnected contact form.

## Missing media and content

## Design problem to solve

Design an original, media-rich `/about-v3` that makes a newly launched two-founder studio feel credible, selective and deeply attentive by proving founder experience and the quality of its interpretation—without copying Symbol Studio, repeating Convenium’s Services/Work/Contact content, exposing AI as the brand story or inventing client success.
