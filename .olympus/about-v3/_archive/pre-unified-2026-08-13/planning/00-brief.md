# About V3 brief

## Target

- Project: Convenium Studio, `005-agency`
- Route/page: `/about-v3`
- Mode: concept and planning only; implementation will be handed to a separate task

## Outcome

- Primary business goal: establish trust in Convenium as a founder-led boutique digital studio for premium and luxury businesses.
- Primary visitor action: submit a qualified project inquiry through an embedded project-fit form.
- Primary audience: discerning owners and senior decision-makers in premium hospitality, real estate, architecture, high-end products and comparable businesses where digital identity affects status and commercial value.
- Success signal: qualified form submissions; secondary future signals are showreel engagement, meaningful page depth and Work/Services cross-link activation.

## Constraints

- Must preserve: Convenium identity, global navigation/footer behavior, factual founder experience, accessibility, mobile clarity and existing framework.
- Must exclude: previous `/about` and `/about-v2` structures, copy and WebGL concepts; public AI-native positioning; fabricated clients, testimonials, metrics or luxury outcomes.
- May change: About-specific layout, dominant surface balance, information architecture, media system and interaction grammar.
- Stack/dependencies: planning should assume the existing Next.js, React, CSS Modules, GSAP/ScrollTrigger and current component system; no new dependency is authorized in this run.
- Deadline/budget: not specified; media may be generated after Gate B through a detailed user-executed guide.

## Positioning truth

- Convenium is a small luxury digital studio that stays deeply involved in a limited number of projects.
- Rustam brings roughly ten years of development experience, including work at Infineon and other companies/agencies, plus design, engineering, motion and WebGL capability.
- Marija brings social-media, graphic-design, negotiation and multilingual client-communication experience.
- Both founders join personal meetings. Language coverage includes German, English, Croatian, Italian, Russian, Turkish and Azerbaijani, subject to final copy verification.
- Discovery is psychology-informed and designed to help clients articulate preferences and motives; public wording must avoid clinical or professional-psychology claims.
- AI remains an internal production method and is not part of the public brand story.
- Convenium has no publishable studio-client history yet. Trust must come from founder experience, clearly labelled independent work, process evidence and service standards.

## Taste references

| Reference | Specifically liked | Do not copy / avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Symbol Studio About | High information value, light editorial design, detailed process explanation, strong media density, deliberate scroll pacing, dedicated showreel, large process modules, strong Services/Projects cross-links, typographic contact footer | Logo, Rules font, orange identity, exact cards/layouts, slogans, project media, Framer structure and unverified proof claims |

## Recommended brand translation

- Keep Convenium’s global header, footer, logo, typography and noir/cotton/cherry identity.
- Make warm cotton/light editorial fields dominant and use noir for the showreel, capability proof, cross-links and closing form/footer.
- Position the showreel after the thesis/founder setup and before the working-method chapters.
- Use an embedded project-fit form near the end, framed as the beginning of a diagnostic conversation.

- Analytics: none supplied.
