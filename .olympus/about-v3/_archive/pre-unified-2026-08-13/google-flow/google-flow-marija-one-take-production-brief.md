References: both Marija identity images plus selected CAL-M. Use `paired-look-reference.png` only if it is face-free.

Quantity: 4. Aspect: 4:5. Intended master: 1600×2000 or highest available.

```text
Create a vertical 4:5 editorial portrait of Marija for the right half of a split-screen boutique studio website opening.

Marija is positioned on the outer-right side and turns slightly inward toward frame-left, as if listening and responding to a collaborator across a central seam. Her expression is attentive, self-assured, and nuanced, not a corporate smile. A few tactile layouts, campaign-sequencing cards, or negotiation notes appear softly out of focus without readable text. Preserve a quiet inner-left 35 percent of the frame for live website typography and the central seam.

Use a natural 50mm or 85mm editorial lens character, eye-level camera, controlled soft light, near-black/charcoal setting, warm-cotton details, restrained grain, and realistic skin. The result must carry equal authority to a paired founder portrait without imitating another person’s posture or identity. Communicate visual judgment, communication, and client understanding without influencer clichés.

The composition must survive a 4:5 master and later responsive crops. Preserve Marija’s exact identity. Apply the global art direction.
```

Acceptance: exact identity; strong inward gaze; inner-left 35% safe area; natural hands/anatomy; no cyan/green cast; eye-level 50/85mm character; controlled grade suitable for pairing.
