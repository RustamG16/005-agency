# Convenium Studio — Logo lockup generation prompts (v3, horizontal lockup)

Colors from `styles/tokens.css`: background = bone `#EEEDE8`, mark + wordmark = noir `#050505`.
Output is now a **horizontal lockup**: icon on the left, the word CONVENIUM on the right. Prompts describe geometry only — no meaning statements. Concept titles are labels for us, not part of the prompt.

## Shared lockup clause (opens every prompt)

> A minimalist flat vector logo lockup on a warm bone off-white background (#EEEDE8), arranged as a single horizontal row: a square icon on the left and the single word "CONVENIUM" on the right. The word is set in one line of uppercase noir (#050505) letters in a heavy geometric sans-serif, evenly letter-spaced, with the word's cap-height block optically centered against the icon's height and its vertical center aligned to the icon's center. A clear even gap separates icon and word, roughly half the icon's width. The icon and the word are the only elements.

## Notes

* Canvas: use a wide/landscape frame (≈16:9 or 2:1) rather than square — a square canvas squeezes a horizontal lockup.
* Icon scale: the icon reads as a square block roughly matching the wordmark's cap height plus a little overshoot. Fine line-count concepts (01, 02, 09) lose detail at small icon size — generate large, downscale later.
* Wordmark: the final build uses Archivo Black. Generators won't match it exactly; treat the generated wordmark as a placement study and reset the type in vector.
* Concept 07 keeps a C inside the icon, so the "no other letters" clause is worded to allow it.
* Inverse variant: swap to bone (#EEEDE8) mark and wordmark on a noir (#050505) background — same prompts with the two hex codes exchanged.
* Gold (#B18A46) stays out of the prompts on purpose: per LOGO-BRIEF, gold is at most one small accent, best added in vector post-work.
* Stacked variant (icon above word) is a separate pass — don't ask one prompt to do both.
