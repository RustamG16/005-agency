# Convenium Studio — Logo mark generation prompts (v2, token-corrected)

Colors from `styles/tokens.css`: background = bone `#EEEDE8`, mark = noir `#050505`.
Prompts describe geometry only — no meaning statements. Concept titles are labels for us, not part of the prompt.

Shared suffix used in every prompt:
> Flat solid color, uniform single stroke weight, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no 3D, no perspective, no text, centered on a square canvas with generous even margin, Swiss-style vector logo mark.

## Notes

- Inverse variant: swap to bone (#EEEDE8) marks on noir (#050505) background — same prompts with the two hex codes exchanged.
- Gold (#B18A46) is not in these prompts on purpose: per LOGO-BRIEF, gold is at most one small accent, best added in vector post-work rather than trusted to a generator.
- Numbers ("sixty", "twenty-four") are directional — generators approximate counts. Exact counts get fixed when the chosen direction is rebuilt as SVG.
